I.
Emerald — the colour of beginning

For a decade, we have been planting walls.

Vertical Green began with a small belief — that a wall could breathe. Ten years on, that belief has rooted across Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines, and into more than five hundred living surfaces.

We are gardeners of the upright world: designing, building, and tending green walls that turn concrete into canopy.

Every story begins as a seed someone refused to forget.

And the city, slowly, learned to exhale.

II.
Canopy — HyGro®Wall

A forest, set vertical.

Our patented HyGro®Wall is a self-irrigating modular system — felt-rooted, quietly fed, indoors or out. Each panel hosts dozens of species; each wall becomes a small ecosystem.

From a single panel in a quiet corner, to a six-storey façade humming with bees, the system scales with the dream.

III.
Sage — HyGro®Moss & Preserve

The quiet colour of memory.

Hand-picked from forests in Northern Europe and naturally preserved, our moss walls hold the hush of woodland inside any room — soft underfoot of the eye, sound-absorbing, never thirsty.

HyGro®Preserve takes it further: real plants, gently kept, for the walls that light forgot.

Some greens do not grow — they remember.

A wall is more than a surface. It is an invitation.

IV.
Verdigris — the colour of return

Where green grows, people grow back.

In hospitals our walls soften the long corridors. In offices like Wise Singapore, they turned a working day into a tropical clearing. In malls and lobbies, they invite a small, surprised exhale.

This is what biophilic design is for — not decoration, but quiet medicine.

NotableWise SG · Healing Walls SpacesOffice · Hospital · Mall
V.
Sunlit Leaf — HyGro®Roof & Landscape

Skylines, blooming.

Above our heads, we are turning roofs into meadows. HyGro®Roof cools buildings, cradles biodiversity, and lets a city remember it was once a forest.

Below them, HyGro®Landscape carries the green down to the street — a continuous, living thread from canopy to kerb.

Imagine the skyline, flowering.

VI.
Hearth Green — the colour of home

A house that breathes.

A single living panel by the kitchen window. A moss inscription above the bed. A hanging mat of ferns at the entrance to greet you, every evening, with the same quiet kindness.

Biophilic design at home is not a luxury — it is a daily, gentle remembering that we, too, are growing things.

Plant a wall. Plant a feeling. Plant a future.
