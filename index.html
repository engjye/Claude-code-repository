Our Mission

We believe a wall can breathe. That a building can bloom.

For over a decade we have transformed concrete into canopy —
connecting people to a greener, healthier, happier environment.

Living Walls

A forest, vertical.

Real plants, rooted in our patented HyGro®Wall system — irrigated, breathing, alive. Every panel is a small ecosystem; every wall a quiet rebellion against grey.

Philosophy

Biophilic design,
beautifully accessible.

Greenery should not be a luxury reserved for postcards and rainforests. It should live in our offices, our homes, our hospitals — anywhere a human breathes.

We design for the senses you forgot you had: the hush of leaves, the cool of moss, the soft green light that makes a room feel like a held breath.

The HyGro® Family

Eight ways to bring nature in.

HyGro®Wall

A living, breathing vertical garden. Self-irrigating modular panels host hundreds of species, indoors or out.

HyGro®Roof

Green roofs that cool buildings, cradle biodiversity, and turn skylines into meadows.

HyGro®Moss

100% preserved moss from Northern European forests — soft, sound-absorbing, no maintenance, forever green.

HyGro®Preserve

Real plants, naturally preserved. The look of life without the thirst — perfect for low-light, hard-to-reach walls.

HyGro®Landscape

Outdoor landscapes that flow from soil to sky. End-to-end design, install and care.

HyGro®Mat & Pot & Artificial

Mats, pots and lifelike artificial greenery for every brief, every budget, every brief space of light.
